# Weekly Status Report

**Course:** Introduction to Software Engineering

**Department:** Software Engineering Department, Faculty of Information and Technology, University of Science

## Objectives

- Report weekly progress against the project plan.
- Record issues and risks.
- Record the weekly status meeting.

## 1. Submission

- Submit one report per week, from **week 2 to week 10** (**9 reports**). The **team lead** (or backup) is responsible.
- The team chooses its own weekly deadline (e.g., the day after the status meeting) and states it in **PA1**.
- Commit the report to the repository at `/docs/management/weekly/W<week>-status.docx` (or `.md`), e.g., `W03-status.md`.
- **Do not overwrite or rewrite earlier reports**; the commit history is evidence of timeliness.
- Reports are **not submitted on Moodle**. They are graded at the end of the course as part of **Teamwork & tool usage** (working process).
